What SayGday is

  • Your questions and answers, written or signed off by you
  • An easy way for customers to find them, in their own words, any time of day
  • A direct line to you when there isn’t an answer yet
  • Yours to change whenever you like

What it isn’t

  • A robot making up replies on the spot
  • Something that learns from your customers’ chats
  • A call centre, or a third party in another country
  • Pretending to be a person

Straight answers

The questions people ask us.

Will it ever make something up?
No. It can only show answers your business has signed off. If there isn’t one, it says so and offers to pass the question on.
Does it learn from my customers?
No. It only changes when you change it. Add, edit or remove answers any time from your dashboard.
Will customers think they’re talking to a person?
It never pretends to be one. It shows your team’s answers, and anything new goes to a real person: you.
Why use AI at all?
Because writing 25 questions and answers from scratch takes hours. AI does the first draft from your own website in minutes, and you do the part that matters: deciding exactly what your customers read.
