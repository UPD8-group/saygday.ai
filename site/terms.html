The short version

  • Your answers are your words, and you have the final say on every one.
  • No lock-in contract. Stop any time.
  • Nothing here takes away your rights under Australian Consumer Law.

  1. Who we are

    SayGday.ai is a business name of HEAR.IS PTY LTD (ABN 37 702 004 608), Canberra, Australia. “We” and “us” means HEAR.IS PTY LTD. “You” means the business that signs up, and the person signing up for it.

  2. What SayGday does

    SayGday puts a chat button on your website. It shows your customers the answers you’ve signed off, word for word. If there’s no answer, it offers to send the question to you. It never makes up an answer.

  3. Your account

    You must be allowed to act for the business and its website. Keep your email account secure, because that’s how you sign in. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has got in.

  4. Your answers are yours

    You own the answers you write and sign off. You let us store them and show them to your customers through your chat button. The answers we draft from your website are suggestions only: please check each one before you sign it off. You’re responsible for the answers you publish.

  5. Your customers’ questions

    When a customer sends a question to your team, we pass it on to you. Replying is up to you. Please use their details only to reply to them.

  6. Not for emergencies

    The chat button isn’t watched by a person around the clock. Don’t rely on it for anything urgent. If your business handles emergencies, put your emergency phone number in your answers.

  7. Fair use

    Don’t use SayGday for anything unlawful, misleading or harmful, for spam, or on a website that isn’t yours. If you do, we may pause your chat button. We’ll tell you why and give you a chance to fix it.

  8. Price and free days

    Your first 14 days are free. After that SayGday costs A$30 a month. There’s no lock-in contract and you can stop any time. We’ll give you at least 30 days’ notice by email before any price change.

  9. Keeping it running

    We work hard to keep SayGday running, but we can’t promise it will never go down. Nothing in these terms excludes your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. As far as the law allows, our total responsibility to you is limited to what you paid us in the last 12 months.

  10. Stopping

    You can close your account at any time. We may close an account after 30 days’ notice, or sooner if these terms are seriously broken. When an account closes, we delete its information as our privacy page explains.

  11. Changes

    If we change these terms, we’ll email you at least 30 days before the change takes effect.

  12. The law that applies

    These terms are governed by the laws of the Australian Capital Territory.
