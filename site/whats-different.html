What’s different

Yes, it’s a chatbot.
Just not like the others.

Most chatbots come up with their own answers on the spot. SayGday only ever says what you’ve already said, so your customers get the same answer you’d give them across the counter.

How we do it

You sign it off. We show it.

  • Your answers are ready before anyone asks.
  • Customers read them word for word.
  • Every answer is signed off by someone at your business.
  • No answer yet? It says so, and passes the question straight to you.

What stays yours

Your words. Nobody else’s.

The same answer, every time

Monday morning or midnight on Sunday, everyone who asks reads the same words. Nothing drifts, and nothing gets reworded.

It sounds like you

They’re your own words: your “no worries”, your “see you Saturday”, your way of putting things.

Nothing you didn’t say

It only shows answers you’ve signed off, so it can’t promise a discount, a booking or a refund you never offered.

Yours to change

Prices go up and hours change. Edit an answer in your dashboard, and that’s what customers read from then on.
