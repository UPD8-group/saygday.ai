Privacy

Your information,
looked after.

What we collect, why we need it, who helps us run SayGday, and how to ask us to change or delete it.

Photo: Santy Sun, Unsplash

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to run your chat button.
  • We never sell your information, or your customers’.
  • What a customer types into the chat stays on their own device, unless they choose to send their question to you.

Who we are

SayGday.ai is a business name of HEAR.IS PTY LTD (ABN 37 702 004 608), based in Canberra, Australia. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988. You can reach us at hello@saygday.ai.

What we collect from business owners

  • Your email address, to sign you in with a code and to send you your customers’ questions.
  • Your business name and website address.
  • The questions and answers you write or sign off, and how your chat button looks.
  • When you ask for a sign-in code, your email and internet address in a scrambled form, so nobody can flood an inbox with codes. We clear it within a day.

What we read from your website

When you ask us to, we read the public pages of your website: the same words any visitor can see. We use them to draft questions and answers for you to check. To write those drafts we send the text of your pages to Anthropic, a company that makes AI software. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, it isn’t used to train their models. Nothing we draft reaches your customers until you sign it off.

Before your chat goes live, we check the website is yours: we look for your chat button on its home page, or for a record you add to your domain’s settings. We keep only the result.

What we collect from your customers

  • Reading answers: we find answers on the customer’s own device. We don’t receive or keep what they type. We only count how often each answer is opened, not who opened it.
  • Asking your team: if the chat can’t answer and the customer chooses to send their question, we keep the question and the email address they give (if they give one), and we email it to you.
  • Stopping spam: to limit how many messages can be sent, we count them by the internet address they come from. We keep only a scrambled form that can’t be turned back into the address, and clear it within a day.

For your customers’ questions, we act on your behalf. Please use their email address only to reply to them.

If you send us a message

When you use the form on our contact page, we keep your name, email address, your message and any business details you add, so we can reply to you and, if you’re joining, help you get set up. We don’t use them for anything else.

Who helps us run SayGday

  • Supabase stores our database, in Sydney.
  • Netlify runs our website and servers, and holds the messages sent through our contact form. Information can pass through its servers in the United States.
  • Resend sends our emails, from the United States.
  • Anthropic drafts answers from your public website text, in the United States.

Each of them handles information only to provide their service to us.

Cookies and storage

Your dashboard keeps you signed in using your browser’s storage. The chat button on your website doesn’t use cookies, and we don’t use advertising or tracking tools.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. If you close it, we delete your business, your answers and your customers’ questions within 30 days.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Email hello@saygday.ai and we’ll look after it. If you’re not happy with how we handled it, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
