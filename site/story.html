Our story

Where SayGday came from.

G’day, I’m James. I’ve spent the last 30 years working in customer service, across education, airlines, hospitality, media and transport.

In every one of those industries, people asked the same questions and looked for the same information. And what really made the difference was the human touch. People feel better when a person has answered them.

It’s one thing to read a sign or be told something. When you can actually have that human interaction, that’s when it becomes a lot more personable.

That’s the idea behind SayGday. It’s still an online chat, but the answers come from real people: every question and answer in it was written by the people who run the business. You’re not sent off to an AI bot, or to a third party in another country. You’re getting their answer.

I make SayGday here in Canberra. I build it and do the first setups myself. Luna and Stormi handle quality control and welcomes.

James
