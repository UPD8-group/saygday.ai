# SayGday — complete content overhaul

Prepared: 16 October 2026

**Status: complete content proposal, not a production release.** This document contains proposed customer-facing copy and clearly separated implementation notes. It does not change the website, activate new prices, create a Managed subscription, change existing customers' agreements, or start any email campaigns. Proposed new-customer prices are A$59/month for SayGday and A$99/month for SayGday Managed. The Managed scope below is a proposed service commitment, not a verified existing feature or operating process.

## 1. Direction and content rules

Sell useful customer answers and control over the business's wording. Do not lead with cheapness, AI terminology, characters, or a claimed saving against another oo.studio product.

Keep the strongest existing headline: **Looks like a chatbot. Answers like you.**

Supporting idea: **Your answers. Ready when customers ask.**

The short explanation: **AI helps with the first draft. You decide what customers read.**

Use natural Australian English: friendly, direct and useful, without forced slang. Write SayGday as the product name and oo.studio as the maker. Use “I” for signed messages from Jay and “we” for the service. Never describe the business as a large support team.

### Claims to use

- SayGday drafts questions and answers from the business's website for owner review.
- Customer-facing business answers come from approved entries, in their approved wording.
- Visitors can ask questions in their own words; the system attempts to match an approved answer.
- A visitor can submit an unanswered question with an email address for follow-up.
- Owners can edit answers and add useful new ones.

### Claims to remove or avoid

- “Spare change”, “about $1 a day”, and “cheap chatbot” positioning.
- “Never wrong”, “perfect accuracy”, “understands every question”, or blanket “never guesses” claims.
- “It passes every unanswered question to you” without explaining that the visitor must submit it.
- Guaranteed sales, conversion gains, time savings or an uptime guarantee.
- “Unlimited” usage, arbitrary message allowances, universal website compatibility, live inventory, bookings, payments, or features not verified and delivered.
- Invented testimonials, customer counts, ratings, savings, urgency or “most popular” badges.
- “Normally $99, yours for $60” comparisons with oo.studio's separate website package.

### Proposed navigation

Primary: How it works · Pricing · Why SayGday · Contact

Account action: Sign in

Primary call to action: Build my SayGday

Keep the existing `/is-this-ai` URL, but label the page “Why SayGday”. Keep character selection as an optional personalisation feature and move “Meet the mob” out of the primary sales journey. Preserve the existing route rather than breaking links.

## 7. Frequently asked questions — reusable across site and SayGday's own chat

These are proposed new-offer answers, not automatically approved entries in a production chatbot. Publish them only after the offer and billing agree. Preserve existing customers' account-specific prices in account messages.

### 1. What is SayGday?

SayGday adds a chat to your website that helps customers find answers you've reviewed and approved. You manage the answers, and visitors can send you an enquiry when they need something you haven't answered yet.

### 2. How is it different from a typical AI chatbot?

SayGday uses approved business answers rather than asking generative AI to write a new response for each visitor. AI helps with the initial draft; you decide which answers are ready to use. The point is control over your wording, not a promise of perfect accuracy.

### 3. Does SayGday write answers for me?

It prepares suggested questions and answers from your website to get you started. You check the details, edit the wording and approve the answers you want customers to see. You can also write your own.

### 4. What happens when it doesn't know the answer?

When SayGday can't find a suitable approved answer, the visitor can submit their question and email address. That gives you a way to reply directly. The visitor needs to choose to send the enquiry—it is not automatically an email lead every time a question goes unanswered.

### 5. How much does it cost?

The proposed new-customer plans are SayGday at A$59/month, or SayGday Managed at A$99/month with an agreed setup and monthly content-support allowance. Both are priced in Australian dollars. Existing agreed arrangements are unchanged.

**Publication edit:** Remove “proposed” from the first sentence only when the new offer is actually live and checkout matches it.

### 6. Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Build and preview the self-managed SayGday without a card. Once you verify your website and activate with a card, you get seven free days. It then renews at A$59/month unless you cancel before your first charge. The free week starts with the confirmed subscription, not account creation.

### 7. What's included in Managed?

Managed includes the SayGday software, a standard setup on one supported website, initial preparation of up to 20 short questions and answers, initial checks, and one monthly content review with small updates within a total 20-minute allowance. You approve the business answers. Jay confirms the scope and start date before you subscribe; more involved work is quoted separately.

### 8. Do I need to move my website?

Not necessarily. SayGday is added to an existing website using installation code. Your site needs to support the embed, and some website-builder plans restrict custom code. Send us your web address when you'd like help checking suitability.

### 9. Does it work on every website platform?

We don't promise that without checking. Compatibility depends on your platform, plan, security settings and ability to add the installation code. Contact us before choosing Managed when you're unsure.

### 10. Can I change the answers or how it looks?

Yes. You can manage the answers in your dashboard and personalise the chat's appearance using the available settings. You can choose a character or keep the button simple. You don't need to use a character to use SayGday.

### 11. Does it update itself whenever my website changes?

Your approved answers need to be kept up to date. Don't assume a change to your website has also changed SayGday. Check the dashboard and update the relevant answers when your information changes.

### 12. Do customers have to create an account?

They can use the website chat without creating a SayGday account. When they want a personal reply to an unanswered question, they need to provide an email address and submit the enquiry.

### 13. Can SayGday take bookings, check stock or accept payments?

That isn't what the current offer promises. It can share your approved information about how to book, buy or get in touch, but it is not itself a live booking, stock or payment system.

### 14. Can I cancel?

Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled. Cancelling stops future renewals; your billing page shows the relevant dates for your account. Cancel the self-managed free trial before your first billing date to avoid the first charge. Contact us about any separately agreed Managed work.

### 15. Who is behind SayGday?

SayGday is made by oo.studio in Canberra. Jay is the person to speak to about setup, support and whether it's a good fit for your business.
