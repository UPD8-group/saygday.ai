# SayGday: first 10 paying businesses

Prepared 8 October 2026. Campaign draft; not launched. Target: 10 distinct external businesses with a successful A$30 recurring payment and an active subscription, representing A$300 monthly recurring revenue before fees. Trials, our own businesses, test payments, failed payments and refunded first payments do not count. A second website owned by the same customer is revenue, but is not a second acquired client.

## Paste into Polsia after signing in

We already operate SayGday.ai at https://saygday.ai. Help us acquire our first 10 paying business clients. Keep the existing website, GitHub repository, hosting, authentication and direct Stripe billing. Your initial remit is growth research and campaign drafts.

SayGday is an interactive FAQ for a business website. It shows only answers the owner has approved, word for word. If it cannot answer, visitors can leave an enquiry for the business. Website scanning drafts answers for owner review; do not describe visitor conversations as AI generated. The product costs A$30 per month per website, with 14 free days and no card required to start. The trial begins when website ownership is verified. Do not advertise features that are not live, including voice, WhatsApp or appointment booking.

Start with independent Canberra pet groomers and pet day-care businesses that have their own website and repeated questions about hours, prices, preparation and booking policies. This is an initial segment to test, not an assertion that it will convert. Research 30 suitable businesses, returning business name, public website, evidence of relevant repeat questions, and a specific demo angle. Use public business information only. Avoid collecting personal contact details.

Prepare three short promotional drafts, a 60-second demo outline and a simple funnel report. Draft outreach for the founder to review; do not send email, direct messages or make calls. Do not publish ads, social posts or change the site yet. Spend limit is zero until an explicit budget and campaign are approved. Do not enable automatic credit top-ups or subscribe to services. Report Polsia's actual subscription/credit cost and ad-management fee before proposing a paid experiment.

Send interested businesses to https://saygday.ai/pricing using source tags for campaign links, and keep checkout inside SayGday. Do not create Polsia payment links or migrate our Stripe account. Do not claim guaranteed savings, revenue or customer outcomes. Do not fabricate testimonials or customer numbers. Keep dashboard visibility private where configurable; do not upload customer enquiries or API secrets.

## First experiment

| Step | Work | Evidence to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ready to sell | Pass test-mode checkout, webhook, cancellation and failed-payment rehearsal; activate the correct live Stripe account | Working payment flow and live smoke checks |
| Find a narrow audience | Review 30 public websites; shortlist the strongest 10 repeat-question use cases | Website, relevant FAQ examples, reason for fit |
| Make the product tangible | Record one demo using our own or explicitly permitted website | Approved answer, unanswered enquiry, owner dashboard |
| First conversations | Founder starts with existing contacts and introductions; review any outbound campaign separately | Permission/source, demo requested, objections |
| Install trials | Help interested owners approve answers and install their button | First verification time and trial end, not just signup |
| Convert | Explain A$30/month before expiry; direct owners to their billing settings | First successful invoice and active subscription |
| Retain | Check usage and whether enquiries are useful after the first paid month | Active paid clients, cancellations, reasons |

Planning hypothesis, not forecast: 100 qualified prospects → 25 demos → 20 verified installations → 10 first payments. Replace these ratios with observed results. Review after the first 10 demos; if fewer than three owners install, fix the audience, offer or setup friction before buying traffic. A full 14-day trial means the first cohort will not produce immediate recurring revenue.

## Polsia setup and costs

The public onboarding has been filled with SayGday.ai and its website and is waiting for the owner's sign-in. No subscription, terms acceptance, publication or advertising purchase has been completed.

Read the actual plan price at checkout before proceeding. Polsia's terms reviewed on 8 October 2026 describe a 20% ad-management fee included within configured ad budgets, and a separate 3% fee plus Stripe processing on customer payments collected through Polsia payment links. Those payment-link terms include a settlement delay. This campaign should use SayGday's own checkout.
